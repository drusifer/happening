## UX & HCI Evaluation

1. **"Nudge, not startle" Principle**:
   - Starting audio at 60 seconds with low volume ($\approx 15\%$) and a relaxed 1.5s tempo (40 BPM) ensures users aren't shocked by sudden loud noises.
   - The exponential acceleration towards 0 seconds creates organic tension without harsh alarm sounds.

2. **User Choice & Control**:
   - Including `enableAudioCountdown` in settings respects user preference and accessibility needs in quiet environments.

3. **Feedback & Clarity**:
   - The classic 1980s Asteroid B1/B2 heartbeat is recognizable and communicates urgency intuitively without needing visual focus on the strip.
