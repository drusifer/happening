# F-32 Audio Countdown Timer — End-to-End UX Verification

**Date**: 2026-09-30 | **Reviewer**: @Smith (UX Advocate) | **Status**: ✅ APPROVED

---

## HCI & Usability Findings

1. **"Nudge, Not Startle" Audio Dynamics**:
   - Initial beat at 60s is soft ($\text{vol} = 0.15$) and calm (40 BPM), perfectly respecting cognitive focus without jarring the user.
   - Accelerating tempo to 500 BPM at $T = 0\text{s}$ creates natural time pressure and urgency intuitively.

2. **User Agency & Accessibility**:
   - The **Asteroid Beats** toggle switch in Settings Panel provides immediate, unambiguous control for quiet or open-office environments.
   - Disabling the toggle or hiding/minimizing the strip silences audio instantly without lag.

3. **Multi-Sensory Cohesion**:
   - Combining the visual scale/shake animation at $T \le 60\text{s}$ with the alternating B1/B2 audio beats creates an immersive, ADHD-friendly time awareness cue.
